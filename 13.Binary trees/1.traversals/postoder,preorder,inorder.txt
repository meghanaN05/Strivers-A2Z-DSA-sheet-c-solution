Preorder  = Root → Left → Right
Inorder   = Left → Root → Right
Postorder = Left → Right → Root
Given	Can find:
Preorder + Inorder	 Postorder
Postorder + Inorder	 Preorder
Preorder + Postorder Not always uniquely possible
* Inorder must usually be present to uniquely construct a binary tree.
